1. Who we are

Summer Content Studio is a private tool operated by [[OPERATOR NAME]] ("we", "us"). It helps one content creator plan, write, schedule and publish her own social media posts. It is not a public service: there are no visitor accounts and no sign-up. Contact for anything in this policy: [[CONTACT EMAIL]].

2. What the service does with your data

The creator connects her own Instagram, TikTok and Fanvue accounts by pressing "Allow" on each platform's own login page. We never see or store a password for those platforms. The service then drafts captions with AI, schedules posts, uploads approved posts (Instagram, Fanvue) or finished videos (TikTok drafts), shows how a post is doing, and lists comments on her posts so she can reply to them herself. On Fanvue it also lists private chats in which a fan is waiting for a reply, drafts a suggested reply with AI, and sends a reply only when the creator presses Send. It shows her Fanvue earnings and subscriber totals, read from Fanvue each time and not stored.

4. AI processing

To write drafts and suggested replies, text (the creator's brief, captions, for reply drafts the text of a comment, and for Fanvue chat drafts the last few messages of that chat) and, if enabled, a few still images taken from her own photos and videos are sent to an AI provider: Anthropic and/or Mistral AI. They process it to produce the text and are not told to keep it for any other purpose of ours. A reply is only ever sent after the creator reads, edits and approves it. Every post is approved by a person before it is published. Messages that need a human (for example a question about whether the creator is real or AI, anything about age, meeting in person, payments or safety) get no AI draft at all.

5. Who else handles data (service providers)

  • Meta (Instagram), TikTok and Fanvue - the platforms the creator connects; their own privacy policies apply to what they hold. Payments and payouts happen entirely inside Fanvue.
  • DigitalOcean - the server the service runs on, including its backups. Cloudflare - domain name service.
  • Anthropic and Mistral AI - AI text and image understanding (section 4).
  • Dropbox - the creator's media is copied from her own Dropbox folder to the server; nothing is changed or deleted in Dropbox.
  • Telegram or ntfy - push messages to the creator's phone, which can contain a caption, the start of a comment, a fan's username, or earnings totals (never the text of a fan's private message).

6. How long we keep it

  • Access tokens: until the account is disconnected (the tokens are then deleted) or the service is closed.
  • Posts, media, comments and statistics: until the creator or we delete them, or the service is closed.
  • Fanvue chat messages: deleted automatically after 90 days. A chat with no stored messages left is deleted too.
  • Server backups: nightly database copies for 14 days, and the hosting provider's snapshots on its own schedule.

7. Security

The service is reached only over HTTPS and sits behind a login. Platform access tokens are encrypted in the database. Access is limited to the operator and the people they authorise. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a problem that affects your data we will tell the people concerned.
